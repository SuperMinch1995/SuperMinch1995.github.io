1969 · Introduced in France
tap to expand
Trimebutine (Débridat), from the French firm Jouveinal, enters use for functional bowel disorders, then spreads via national authorisations across Europe, Latin America and Asia.
Source: in clinical use since 1969 (PMID 10918948; doi:10.1055/s-0031-1300246).
1978–1980 · Controlled IBS trials
Lüttecke reports controlled trials in irritable colon, anchoring trimebutine's European clinical use.
Source: Lüttecke, Curr Med Res Opin 1980 (PMID 6988171); J Int Med Res 1978.
1987 · Phase III–like activity in infants
Boige et al. record trimebutine-induced phase III–like activity in 4 of 5 infants with severe dysmotility — 2 with pseudo-obstruction.
Source: Boige et al., J Pediatr Gastroenterol Nutr 1987;6:548–553 (PMID 3430262).
1993 · Warner-Lambert takes a stake
Warner-Lambert acquires 34% of Jouveinal, with rights to develop its products for markets outside France.
Source: Warner-Lambert / Jouveinal, Jan 1993 (Scrip/citeline).
2000 · Pfizer acquires Warner-Lambert
Trimebutine enters Pfizer's portfolio through the acquisition.
Source: Pfizer–Warner-Lambert, 2000.
From 1969 onward, the United States appears nowhere on this timeline — no NDA on record, never FDA-approved.
