A new wire in a known circuit

Intestinal tuft cells already run a chemosensory cascade that turns luminal cues into type 2 immunity. Elmentaite et al. propose a second input — IgG detected by Fc receptors — that would feed the same downstream machinery. The two arms converge on a shared calcium/TRPM5 hub.
